### 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
This agreement establishes the terms for developing a comprehensive Multi-Branch ERP and Financial Management System for Newgen Online School (Pvt) Ltd to manage daily branch income, expenses, retail sales, customer credit, bank accounts, and multi-tier verification workflows.

---

### 2. SCOPE OF WORK & CORE FEATURES

1. Multi-Branch Operations & Cash Drawers: Real-time physical cash tracking and chronological running balances for each operational branch (Danuma, Arunalu, Vition, Matale, Ankumbura, Online School, etc.).
2. Financial Ledger & Verification Queue: Daily income and expense entries with auto-generated bill numbers, 3-state workflow (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), and verifier audit workbench.
3. Inter-Branch Cash Settlement Engine: Collect payments at one branch on behalf of another, with physical cash handover, company bank deposit, or direct mutual offset workflows.
4. Retail & Communication Sales: Rapid barcode/item sales with automated profit calculation and telecom operator classification (Dialog, Mobitel, Airtel, Hutch).
5. Customer Credit Accounts & Statements: Manage credit lines, record credit sales, process cross-branch debt repayments, and generate comprehensive customer statements.
6. Multi-Bank Account Reconciliation: Track institutional bank accounts (BOC, Commercial Bank, etc.), monitor deposits/withdrawals, and maintain real-time bank ledger balances.
7. Petty Cash Float Management: Branch petty cash allocation, daily expenditure recording, replenishment request workflows, and balance tracking.
8. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Distinct dashboards, least-privilege permissions, and middleware route security for Admin, Verifier, and Branch Staff.
9. Immutable Audit Trail & Security: Automatic logging of all critical actions (approvals, rejections, amount overrides, role changes, settlements) with actor, timestamp, and audit remarks.
10. Executive Analytics & Reporting Suite: Real-time KPI summary cards, branch-wise comparison charts, revenue vs. expense breakdowns, and instant CSV data export capabilities.
